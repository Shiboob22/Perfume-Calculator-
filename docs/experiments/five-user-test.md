# Five-user test: sign-up → first batch

Goal: see where a new DIY blender stalls between opening the site and logging
a first batch. Five people find most of the big problems.

## Who

Five people who dilute fragrance oil with alcohol (or want to): 3 reading
English, 2 Arabic. Each on their own phone, never signed in to Kyphira before.
Not friends who already saw the app.

## Setup

- Production site, their phone, their own email (no test account).
- Ask them to think aloud. Record the screen if they agree; otherwise notes.
- 20 minutes. Don't help. If stuck for 60 s, note it, then give the smallest hint.
- Afterwards, delete nothing: their account is a real one.

## Tasks (read one at a time)

1. "You found this site from a friend. What is it for?" (home page only, 30 s)
2. "Make a 50 mL bottle at EDP strength. How many grams of oil?"
3. "Save that batch so you can find it later." (sign-up, onboarding, Log)
4. "When will this bottle be ready to wear?"
5. "Would you pay for Pro? What would make you?"

## Watch for

| Task | Note |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do they say "calculator", "journal", "perfume"? Do they scroll or leave? |
| 2 | Find the bottle and strength buttons? Read 50 mL / 20 % correctly? |
| 3 | Where they hit the sign-in wall; email link vs Google; does onboarding feel long; do they find Log (name needed: `calc.logNeedsName`)? |
| 4 | Do they find the Journal tab, the rest line or the 90-day mark? |
| 5 | Their words, not a yes/no. |

## After each session

- Time from opening the site to a logged batch (or where they gave up).
- The single worst moment, in their words.
- Language and phone model.

## Read the five together

List the stalls that happened to 2 or more people. Fix the worst one, write it
in the log in `README.md`, then compare activation before and after.
