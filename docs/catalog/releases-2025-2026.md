# Releases of 2025 and 2026 added by hand

Migration `0037_catalog_releases_2025_2026.sql`, 2026-11-14. Thirty-two rows, source `curated`.

## How each row was checked

The owner chose curated entry over a scraped or licensed source. Each release was looked up by web search and kept only when the house's own product page or press material, or an authorised retailer carrying the house's copy, states its name, year and notes. The house sites could not be opened from the build session (its network allows no outside hosts), so the pages were read through search results; the links below are the pages those results came from.

"House" in the table means the house's own page or press material gave the notes. "Retailer" means the notes come from an authorised retailer's listing and the house's page was not among the results.

What is stored: name, house, year, gender, perfumer where credited, notes. Not stored: photos, ratings, vote counts. The accords and the olfactory group on each row are Kyphira's own reading of the house's description, written in the catalogue's vocabulary, so the Selector, the Nose and the family rule can use the row. Where the house lists notes without a pyramid, they are stored as one list.
