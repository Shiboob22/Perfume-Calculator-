Guide II of III · 8 min

The calculation

From a bottle and a strength to two numbers on a scale.

On this page

A perfume's strength is a share of oil by volume. Your scale reads weight. The calculation is the bridge: turn a share of millilitres into grams, using each liquid's density.

iThe formula

For a bottle of V millilitres at strength s, the oil takes V × s and the ethanol takes the rest.

oil_g    = V × s × ρoilethanol_g = V × (1 − s) × ρethanol

iiA worked example

50 mL at 25%, with the default densities of 0.95 and 0.81 g/mL.

LiquidVolumeρWeigh
Fragrance oil12.50 mL0.9511.88 g
Ethanol 96%37.50 mL0.8130.38 g
Precision rule 3 / 5 Round only at the end. Carry every decimal until the scale reads it.

iiiYour own densities

Your supplier's sheet may list a different density. Enter it in the calculator's overrides; the method stays the same.

ivSafety

Ethanol is highly flammable. Work away from flame and heat, in a ventilated room. Cap the bottle between pours.
