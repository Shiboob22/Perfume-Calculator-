1a · Rationale

  1. A book that came to life: hairline rules, roman numerals, fleurons, square corners, no cards or shadows.
  2. Numbers are the hero: the gram readout is the largest type on screen, set in Plex Mono with tabular figures.
  3. Scale contrast does the luxury work: 96px Poppins headline over a Lora reading column.
  4. Forest green is spent once per view, on the primary action.
  5. Amber is used only to show oil (in the bottle, the ratio bar and the legend), never for text, so it always means "oil".
  6. Accord bars become one bar: oil to alcohol by weight.
  7. Motion: one easing curve, cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1). Controls take 180 ms, the pour and digit roll 460–620 ms, staggered. Only transform is animated, and nothing moves when reduced motion is on.
