The laboratory room at dusk
Rationale

Kyphira reads as a working notebook, not a shop. The ground is warm, near-black stone with two raised surfaces; resin amber is the only accent, so it always means action, progress or heat. Newsreader carries headings because it can be literary without luxury pastiche. Hanken Grotesk runs the UI with tabular figures, so grams line up in columns. Noto Naskh Arabic and IBM Plex Sans Arabic match their Latin partners in weight and stroke, and Arabic gets more line height rather than smaller type. Western numerals throughout keep a formula identical in both languages. Roman numerals, catalogue numbers and 1 px hairlines replace ornament. Motion is quiet everywhere except three places: the homepage hero, Library openers and the Salon profile header.
